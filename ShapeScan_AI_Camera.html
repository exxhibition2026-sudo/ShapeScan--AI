Shape examination

Learning worksheet

After capturing or uploading an object, use these prompts to examine its geometry.

1. What shape do you see?

Examples: cube, cuboid, sphere, cylinder, cone, pyramid, prism.

2. What features can you observe?

Count visible faces, edges and vertices. Some features may be hidden from the camera.

3. Estimate its dimensions

Measure length, width and height with a ruler or a known-size reference object.

4. Your observation

Important: This starter version provides camera capture and an educational worksheet. It does not yet automatically recognize shapes or calculate real-world dimensions. Those features need a computer-vision model and, for measurements, camera calibration or a reference scale.
